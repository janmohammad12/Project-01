The Evolution of Digital Reading

Typography on the web has come a long way since the early days of the internet. Today, designers have unprecedented con trol over how text appears, thanks to modern CSS capabilities and variable fonts.

"Good typography is invisible. Great typography is unforgettable." — Anonymous

Key Principles for Readability

Line length, font choice, and spacing work together to create an enjoyable reading experience. Aim for 45-75 characters per line for optimal readability on desktop screens.
